Of Blight and Bezoars

💉 Piro 📍 Лазареты и кухни ⭐ Сложность: Средняя
While Piro tests the water samples, he has asked me to fetch him several other ingredients. He wants Bezoars, Hartshorn, and Arsenic. He will use them to test blood against the Bacterium. He believes the camp infirmaries might possess what we need. For the poison, he pointed me to old kitchens or hunters' cabins.

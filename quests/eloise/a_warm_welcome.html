A Warm Welcome

👩 Eloise 📍 The Chateau ⭐ Сложность: Лёгкая
After managing to make it inside the Chateau on this Godforsaken island, I met Eloise - the young woman by the main gate. She's advised me to start introducing myself to the others here in this place known as the Outer Ramparts.
The first person I must talk to is a child named Louis. Inexplicably, it seems he is in charge of organising the Stash for those who go back to the mainland on Expeditions.
Afterwards, I must speak with a Leper named Piro. He has a number of items for sale that will come in handy.
Finally, I should talk to Reynauld - up at the top of the hill behind Louis' camp. He'll organise an Expedition to the mainland when I'm ready to go.

🧭 Прохождение

  • Шаг 1: Поговорить с Louis.
  • Шаг 2: Поговорить с Piro.
  • Шаг 3: Поговорить с Reynauld.
  • Шаг 4: Вернутся (поговорить) с Eloise.

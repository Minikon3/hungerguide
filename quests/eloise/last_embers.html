Last Embers

👩 Eloise 📍 Jacques Bridge ⭐ Сложность: Лёгкая
Eloise has asked me to gather charcoal to keep the fires burning for those on the Ramparts. With the cold settling in, she fears many may not make it through the night without warmth. She's mentioned the smithy, old forges, stoves and abandoned camps as good places to search. If all else fails, she suggested that Cutter's Hamlet in Sombre Forest will have large amounts of charcoal.
